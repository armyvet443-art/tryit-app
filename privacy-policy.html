Privacy Policy for TryIt

Last Updated: May 13, 2026
Contact: armyvet443@gmail.com

TryIt is a social app for sharing what you tried. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Account Info: email, username, display name
  • Profile Info: avatar photo, bio, website, Instagram/TikTok/YouTube links, cover photo
  • Your Content: photos, videos, titles, descriptions you post
  • App Activity: posts, Tried posts, Try Later saves, Try Crew Top 8, followers/following, streaks, views
  • Device Info: device type, OS version, IP, crash logs
  • Camera & Photos: only accessed when you choose to take or upload a photo - never in background

How We Use It

To run the app, show your profile and posts in Fair Feed, keep your streaks, and keep the app secure.

How We Share It

We do NOT sell your data. Your username, avatar, and posts are public to other TryIt users. We use Supabase for database/auth and Google Play services. We share if required by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Passwords are hashed. Stored securely in Supabase.

Your Rights

You can edit your profile in Settings, delete your posts, and delete your account anytime by emailing armyvet443@gmail.com. We delete your data within 30 days.

Children

Not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from under 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here.
