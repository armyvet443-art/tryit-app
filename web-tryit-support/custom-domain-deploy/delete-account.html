Delete Your TryIt Account

App: TryIt (com.tryitapp.app) · Developer: TryIt App
Last updated: November 25, 2026
You can delete your TryIt account and its associated data at any time, either inside the app or by emailing us from this page. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

Option 1: Delete your account in the TryIt app

  1. Open TryIt and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Forever to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (no app needed)

Email us from the email address you used to sign up for TryIt, with the subject Delete My Account. Include your TryIt username if you know it.

Email tryitofficialapp@gmail.com

If you can no longer access that email address, tell us in your message and we will ask you for information to confirm the account is yours before deleting it. We will reply within 2 business days and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete:

  • Your sign-in account, including your email address and password login.
  • Your profile: username, display name, bio, profile and cover photos, and social links.
  • Your posts (Tries), including their photos, videos, captions, locations you typed, and ratings, plus the comments, likes, reactions, saves and "Tried" marks on those posts.
  • Comments, likes, reactions, votes, saves, Try Later items and "Tried" marks you made on other posts.
  • Direct messages you sent and photos you sent in chat, your conversation memberships and read receipts.
  • Your stored media files: photos, videos, chat images, profile and cover images, and share images (share cards and watermarked images of your posts, including ones other people made, and ones you made of other people’s posts) are deleted from our storage.
  • Your follows and followers, Try Crew, blocks and mutes, Try With Me invites and collaborations, try requests and results, and passport progress.
  • Your notifications, push notification tokens, settings, recent searches, and the viewing and watch-time records linked to your account.
  • Reports you submitted.

What may be kept, and why

  • Messages other people sent you stay in their copy of the conversation, because they belong to the other person.
  • Safety records: reports other people made about your content or account, and moderation actions, are kept with your account link removed so we can keep the community safe and prevent abuse.
  • Anonymous browsing data: if you used TryIt without signing in, watch-time and voting records tied only to a random app ID on your device (not to your account) are not linked to you and may be kept. Uninstalling the app removes that ID from your device.
  • Crash reports and backups: Crash and error reports may be retained for up to 30 days under our error-monitoring provider’s current plan. Database backups may be retained for up to 7 days. We do not restore deleted accounts from these copies.
  • We may keep information longer if the law requires it or to resolve a legal claim.

How long it takes

  • In the app: your account and the data listed above are deleted immediately.
  • By email: we reply within 2 business days and complete verified requests within 30 days.
  • Remaining crash-report and database-backup copies expire on their normal schedule: crash and error reports within up to 30 days, database backups within up to 7 days.
  • Cached copies of deleted images may take a few minutes to disappear from our content delivery network.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete your own posts and comments in the app at any time. To ask us to delete other specific data without closing your account, email tryitofficialapp@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request.
